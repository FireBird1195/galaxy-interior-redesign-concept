How fees work

No fee that grows with your bill.

Many design firms charge a percentage of what the project costs, so the fee climbs every time the budget does. Galaxy Interior doesn’t charge percentage-based fees or commissions. Materials are chosen to fit the budget you set, and you keep control of it.

The usual wayGalaxy Interior
A fee that rises as a percentage of costNo percentage-based fees or commissions
Materials decided for youMaterials picked to suit your budget

How it works

Three steps, one team.

From the first visit to the last fitting, the same people carry the project through.

  1. Step 01

    Show us your space

    Visit the studio at Gorewada Square or share your plan. Someone from the team notes your requirements, answers your questions and helps you settle on a budget.

  2. Step 02

    Get custom design plans

    Your rooms are designed in 2D and 3D, with a walkthrough video of the finished design. Adjust it until it’s right.

  3. Step 03

    Select & execute

    Choose materials that fit your budget. The team then carries out the civil work, carpentry and finishing, and hands the space over.
