Upgrade the laminate. Not the design fee.

Many designers charge a percentage of the project cost. Choose a better shutter or add a room, and their fee rises with it. Galaxy Interior doesn’t charge percentage-based fees or commissions, and materials are chosen to fit the budget you set.

How a percentage fee grows: a worked example

First planAfter upgrades
Project cost₹8,00,000₹11,00,000
Designer’s fee at 10%₹80,000₹1,10,000
Extra fee for the same design₹30,000

Illustration only. The figures are an example, not a Galaxy Interior quote, and percentage rates vary between firms.

With Galaxy Interior there is no percentage line on the bill.

Three steps, and what you hold after each

  1. 1

    Show us your space

    Visit the showroom or share your floor plan. Someone from the team writes down your rooms and needs, answers questions and helps you settle on a budget.
