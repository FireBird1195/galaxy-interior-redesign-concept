How it works

Three simple steps

  1. Show us your space

    Visit our showroom or share your floor plan. We note your requirements, answer your questions and help you choose a budget.

  2. Get custom design plans

    We design your rooms in 2D and 3D and share a walkthrough video. We refine it with you until it's right.

  3. Select and execute

    Choose materials that suit your budget. Our team then handles civil work, carpentry and finishing through to handover.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you charge a percentage of the project cost?

No. Galaxy Interior does not charge percentage-based fees or commissions, so choosing better materials does not raise a design fee.

Will I see the design before work starts?

Yes. You get 2D layouts, 3D designs and a walkthrough video of your space, and can ask for changes before execution begins.

Can you work to my budget?

Yes. Someone from the team helps you settle on a budget at the first meeting, and materials are chosen to fit it.

What work do you handle?

Design, civil and finishing work, false ceilings, painting, wallpaper, electrical and glass work, modular kitchens and custom furniture, through to handover.

Do you design offices and shops?

Yes: commercial interiors, retail design and event design, as well as homes.

Do you offer Vastu consultancy?

Yes, Vastu consultancy is one of the services the studio offers.
