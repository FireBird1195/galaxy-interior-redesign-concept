Modular kitchens · Nagpur

Kitchens, planned around the corner.

An L-shaped layout puts the corner to work and links cooking, preparation and storage, so the room does more with the space it has.

Why the L works

Three jobs, two walls.

The L keeps the floor open and the three things you do most within a step or two of each other.

  1. 1Storage along the long wall: tall units, drawers, the fridge.
  2. 2Cooking on the corner run, with the chimney above.
  3. 3Preparation and washing by the window, in daylight.

Illustrative plan. Every Galaxy kitchen is drawn to the actual room.

What a kitchen project covers

From the first sketch to the last handle.

The same three steps as every Galaxy project: show us the space, see it designed, then select and execute.

  • A visit or your floor plan to begin
  • Kitchen layout in 2D and 3D
  • A walkthrough video before work starts
  • Modular cabinets and storage, made to measure
  • Counters, backsplash and shutters chosen to your budget
  • Electrical, glass and painting work alongside, if the brief needs it
  • Fitting and handover by the same team
